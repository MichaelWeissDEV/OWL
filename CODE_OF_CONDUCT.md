# Code of Conduct

OWL is a small open-source project. We want it to be a friendly, harassment-free place for
everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Our standards

Expected:

- be respectful and constructive; assume good faith;
- give and accept feedback graciously, and focus on the work, not the person;
- be patient with newcomers and with maintainers (most of us do this in our spare time);
- credit others' work.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal or political attacks;
- publishing others' private information;
- sexualised language or attention;
- sustained disruption of discussions or spamming issues and pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces - issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews - and to
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this code and may block people who
do not follow it. Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the
[GitHub profile of the repository owner](https://github.com/MichaelWeissDEV). Reports are handled
confidentially.

This text is a short, project-specific statement inspired by common open-source codes of conduct.
